## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

**Showroom** is a powerful customization tool for Windows that allows you to manage your desktop wallpaper automatically. With the **latest version**, you can effortlessly change your wallpaper and give your desktop a fresh look without lifting a finger. Simply specify the folder containing your favorite images and the duration each wallpaper should be displayed, and Showroom will take care of the rest!

This small utility not only allows you to modify your desktop wallpaper dynamically but also ensures that every image fits perfectly on your screen. With an intuitive interface and comprehensive functionality, downloading Showroom will elevate your desktop experience.
